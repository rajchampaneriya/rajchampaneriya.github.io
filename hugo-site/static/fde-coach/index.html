RajC FDE Impromptu Coach

RajC FDE Impromptu Coach is a personal practice tool that runs on my own Mac. Every day it shows me five impromptu speaking questions, records my answers on video, and uploads the recording to my own YouTube channel as a private video. It can also add reminder events to my own Google Calendar when I miss a practice day.

RajC FDE Impromptu Coach is used only by its author and is not offered to anyone else.

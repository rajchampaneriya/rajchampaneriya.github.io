RajC FDE Impromptu Coach – Privacy policy

RajC FDE Impromptu Coach runs only on its author's Mac and has no servers.

  • YouTube (youtube.upload): used only to upload practice videos recorded on the Mac to the author's own channel as private videos. No YouTube data is read, stored or shared.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): used only to create and delete this app's own missed-practice reminder events. Other events are not read, stored or shared.
  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the author's Mac, readable only by that user.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
